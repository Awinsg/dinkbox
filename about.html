ABOUT DINK BOX

Your place to play, book, and enjoy pickleball.

Who We Are

Dinkdingk Box is a pickleball court reservation system designed to make booking a court simple and convenient. Players can check court availability, choose a schedule, and reserve their preferred court.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide players with an easy way to find and reserve pickleball courts without having to go through a complicated booking process.

Our Mission

Dink Box aims to make pickleball more accessible by providing a simple and organized court reservation experience for players.
